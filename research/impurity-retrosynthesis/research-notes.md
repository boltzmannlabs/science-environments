# Research notes: evidence-gated impurity retrosynthesis

## Objective and historical investigation

The requested planning basis is 100 g *isolated* target per impurity, ≥95% purity, estimated cost in INR. The targets are ibuprofen EP impurity A (66622-47-7), ibuprofen EP impurity M (60057-62-7), and a putative “osimertinib impurity A” (1421373-98-9). PubChem name/CAS lookups provisionally match A and M; neither establishes an authoritative stereochemical/form specification. The osimertinib CAS maps to AZ5104/metabolite M6/Impurity 3; the label A remains unresolved. Patent WO2013014448A1 Example 59 names the N-H-indole acrylamide corresponding by name to that CAS; Example 28 is the N-methylindole parent. Do not infer that Example 59 proves the label “Impurity A.” [R1–R4]

Two authorized Boltzmann retrosynthesis jobs returned A: 12 paths, 38 labelled reaction nodes and M: 7 paths, 21 nodes. An RDKit root-SMILES comparison matched all 19 to their *provisional* target graphs. All 59 node-level `uspto_link` and procedure fields were empty, so the exports provide no traceable step citations or executable conditions. Ten nodes had an element absent from *listed* precursors; unlisted reagents may supply it, so this is not proof of reaction imbalance. Model scores, step-yield and `path_cost` fields cannot be called measured isolated yields or India 100 g costs. No chemistry experiment, analytical purity assay, supplier quote or osimertinib model run occurred. The original profile-scoped JSON exports and job IDs are not published here; see sanitized `../../environments/impurity-retrosynthesis/results/`.

## New literature changes the decision

CN108794319B is a direct A-product patent: its abstract describes methyl-ester precursor methylation, isobutylboronic-acid Suzuki aryl coupling and ester hydrolysis [R5]. The English example extraction is internally inconsistent (starting-material name versus product; coupling reagent and hydrolysis base missing from example excerpts) and should be verified against original-language figures and primary lab records. Patent-reported example yields are not measured in this run and do not validate a model route or a 100 g batch. WO2003043625A1 Example 8 explicitly names the (R,S) meta-isobutylphenylpropionic acid; its general method involves Stille coupling, hydrogenation and cleavage [R6]. The generated A top5 resembles the boronic coupling class but uses a different protected substrate; top3 shares hydrogenation/hydrolysis topology with WO but not its organotin substrate. Neither is an exact reproduced literature route. Earlier exact-CAS-only searches missed these named patent examples; search by chemical name and precursor connectivity.

ACS Catalysis 2014 [R7] has verified bibliographic metadata for a Sonogashira/regioselective hydration isomer method but primary full text was inaccessible. The bare “*Synthesis* 1983 p. 444” fragment was not uniquely verified. For M, Bellezza et al. [R8] studies alpha-fluoro ester hydrolysis, not the alpha-hydroxy product; Aramini et al. [R9] describes deoxygenated aryl acids from cyanohydrins, so M top4's alpha-OH retention is not established. Kuchar 1984 and Ramesh/Reddy 2007 cannot be treated as exact M product precedents without identifiable primary texts. Cross et al. [R10] establishes AZD9291 pharmacology context, not impurity-A identity or an industrial route. Reference status and links are recorded separately in `references.md`.

## Current decision and falsifiable next checks

No preferred route/backup or low/base/high INR total is justified. Literature is stronger than the generated export in direct A-product evidence; generation was better only at enumerating potential alternative *graphs*, not at measured yield, purity, safety or economics. Prioritize (documentary, not experimental) A top5 versus exact patent Suzuki substrate, A top3 versus exact WO Stille substrate, and M top2/top4 versus condition-specific compatibility and OH retention. Seek accurate primary texts and dated India-context supplier packs, then verify each step's complete atom mapping and product/impurity outcomes. Resolve the osimertinib impurity label with an authoritative reference standard before model submission. Any wet-lab work requires a qualified safety/process review; passing unit tests only proves code contract behavior.
