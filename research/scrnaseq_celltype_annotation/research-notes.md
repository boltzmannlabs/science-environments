# scRNA-seq cell type annotation — research notes

## Research scope

The environment specification proposes cell population identification, marker-gene evidence and cell-type annotation from scRNA-seq inputs, with uncertainty reported where supported.

## Current evidence

The [environment specification](../../environments/scRNAseq-environment/ENVIRONMENT_SPEC.md) names a 10x Genomics PBMC 3k dataset and the Scanpy PBMC 3k tutorial as candidate inputs and workflow references. No new analysis or benchmark results are recorded in this research note.

## Open work
