# Enzyme Variant Optimization References

## Starting enzyme

- [RCSB PDB 1TQH](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1TQH):
  experimental Est30 structure, sequence/entry metadata and covalent-intermediate
  context. The entry is a starting reference, not a prepared MHET complex.
- [Liu et al., 2004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2004.06.069):
  Covalent reaction intermediate revealed in crystal structure of the
  Geobacillus stearothermophilus carboxylesterase Est30.

## Native execution and integration

- [Agent-Scientist](https://github.com/boltzmannlabs/Agent-Scientist):
  native runtime and scientific integrations.
- [Saved specialists](https://github.com/boltzmannlabs/Agent-Scientist/blob/main/website/docs/user-guide/features/saved-specialists.md):
  profile-local review, activation, delegation and persistence.
- [ToolUniverse](https://github.com/mims-harvard/ToolUniverse):
  scientific operation discovery and integration. A catalogue entry alone does
  not establish an available execution service.

## Withheld reference material

Winning engineered sequences, outcome tables and the target optimization
procedure are intentionally outside the design task. They are for later
reference evaluation, not starting inputs for either arm. This is an information
separation policy, not an assertion of enforced isolation or unknown training data.
