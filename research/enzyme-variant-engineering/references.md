# Sources — native enzyme variant environment

1. Carlin DA, Hapig-Ward S, Chan BW et al. (2017). Thermal stability and kinetic
   constants for 129 variants of a family 1 glycoside hydrolase reveal that enzyme
   activity and stability can be separately designed. *PLOS ONE* 12(5): e0176255.
   [Article](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0176255),
   [S1 table](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?type=supplementary&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0176255.s001),
   [S1 methods](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?type=supplementary&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0176255.s003).
   Primary source for reference experimental endpoints, conditions and trade-offs.
   Article/data attribution applies; no new wet-lab work is claimed.
2. Carlin DA, Caster RW, Wang X et al. (2016). Kinetic characterization of 100
   glycoside hydrolase mutants enables the discovery of structural features
   correlated with kinetic constants. *PLOS ONE* 11(1): e0147596.
   [Article](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0147596),
   [S1 code/model archive](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article/file?type=supplementary&id=10.1371/journal.pone.0147596.s001).
   The archive member `S1 Code/bagel.pdb` supplies the study-numbered modelling core.
3. RCSB PDB [2O9P](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2O9P),
   [original mmCIF](https://files.rcsb.org/download/2O9P.cif),
   [UniProt reference record](https://data.rcsb.org/rest/v1/core/uniprot/2O9P/1).
   These document P22505, tag/numbering differences and engineered template changes.
   The entry must not be silently substituted for the study model or assay parent.
4. Native Agent-Scientist [saved specialists](https://github.com/boltzmannlabs/Agent-Scientist/blob/main/website/docs/user-guide/features/saved-specialists.md),
   [delegation](https://github.com/boltzmannlabs/Agent-Scientist/blob/main/website/docs/user-guide/features/delegation.md),
   [Boltzmann instructions](https://github.com/boltzmannlabs/Agent-Scientist/blob/main/plugins/boltzmann/skills/boltzmann-tools/SKILL.md).
   Implementation reviewed at `3d49855e3329d2f2795fac1f0602e258a4806304`.
   Tool contracts and enzyme validators remain owned by that installation.
5. This repository's [README](../../README.md) and
   [contribution guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). They define the environment,
   research, task, test, result and manual-review responsibilities.

Molecular method selection is intentionally left to the agent and current native
catalogue. The case does not imply that every available predictor supports its
substrate, construct, assay conditions or functional-stability endpoint.
