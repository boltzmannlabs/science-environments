## Scientific interpretation

Predicted routes, structural/stock closure, literature-supported steps and measured outcomes are distinct. A source locator or returned tool score alone cannot establish exact reaction support, calibrated feasibility or experimental success. Imported legacy flags cannot upgrade predictions. Unknown costs, quantities and conditions remain explicit.

The environment supports route search, optional recipe-level yield prediction and experimental specialist advice. Main-only remains the default; the available development studies do not demonstrate general benefit from delegation. Custom-model development is deferred in favor of the existing configurable predictor.

## Remaining research

Complete frozen held-out comparison of the strongest fixed workflow, main Agent Scientist and a selected specialist treatment. Compare matched-budget and separately reported unbudgeted regimes, preserve failed runs, and report negative findings. Establish independent reference stewardship and source-based chemical assessment before claiming improved feasible-route retrieval or ranking. AI-assisted review is not independent experimental validation.

All five application interfaces have software paths; real scientific validity remains application-specific. Process examples contain illustrative costs, impurity outputs are hypotheses, biocatalytic source matching does not establish enzyme activity, and condition predictions are limited by the predictor's domain.
