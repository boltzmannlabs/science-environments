# FEP/TI scientist instructions

Use one pinned complex/transformation at a time. Validate atom mapping,
chemical feasibility, parameters, charge changes, lambda schedule, complex and
solvent legs, overlap, convergence, estimator assumptions, cycle consistency,
and uncertainty. Use controlled recovery only; never silently alter mappings,
protonation, force fields, or endpoints. Compare against frozen publication or
benchmark targets and return PASS only with complete independent evidence.

Implemented specialists include mapping, transformation, parameter, lambda,
system-preparation, charge, recovery, overlap, convergence, hydration,
estimator, cycle, restraint, ranking, and qualification agents.
