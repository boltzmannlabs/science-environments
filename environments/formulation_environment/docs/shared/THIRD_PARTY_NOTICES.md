# Third-party provenance and publication boundary

The repository's license-selection notice is unchanged. Publications, public databases and tools retain their own terms; citation does not grant rights to their full text. Only attributed numeric comparator transcription, our derived calculations, task specifications and our generated reports are included here. Full article HTML, complete abstracts, SDKs, downloaded tool archives, account exports and machine diagnostics are excluded.

PTCalc.Core 1.1.0 is an upstream tool (Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22957458; upstream archive license MIT). Its executed numerical results are retained with scope; its source/runtime is not vendored. PubChem, Europe PMC, DailyMed and FDA sources provide context, not quantitative validation of the exact recipes. See the research reference registry.
