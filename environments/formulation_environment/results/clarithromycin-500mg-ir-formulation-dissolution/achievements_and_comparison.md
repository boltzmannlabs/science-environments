# Achievements and honest comparison

## What was actually achieved

24 initial composition-valid designs, expanded to 28, with exact dose/ingredient/core-coat checks. Actual PTCalc analysis of published means; independent arithmetic; identification of an unreproduced source f2 and a KP point-selection fallback. Portable v2 diagnostics and a nine-design interaction-capable experiment plan.

## Quantified change — our model versus our model

| Comparison | Before RMSE, pp | After RMSE, pp | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Weibull free → capped, withheld 30 min | 3.3595 | 2.0197 | 39.88% lower error |
| Logistic free → capped, withheld 30 min | 1.8817 | 1.8151 | 3.54% lower error |
| First-order free → capped, LOTO | 5.6009 | 5.3895 | 3.77% lower error |
| Weibull free → capped, LOTO | 6.4667 | 6.7786 | 4.82% worse |
| Logistic free → capped, LOTO | 5.0964 | 5.3697 | 5.36% worse |

The 30-minute fold is a subset of LOTO. Literature values are the comparison target, not an inferior competing method. No formulation, dissolution, stability, manufacturability or therapeutic improvement over literature was established. Reported values above 100% were not clipped or retrospectively renormalized.

## Remaining gates

Reference product/batch and approved method; API form/PSD/potency and excipient lots; analytical specificity/recovery and sampling corrections; actual manufacture and independent repeats; compatibility and packaged stability; external formulation/batch validation and calibrated uncertainty. Do not promote a planned batch or a lower in-sample error to these evidence tiers.
