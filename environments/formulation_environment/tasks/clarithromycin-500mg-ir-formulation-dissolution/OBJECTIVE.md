# Objective and acceptance — Task - 01

Title: **Clarithromycin 500 mg immediate-release formulation and dissolution investigation**.

## Scientific objective

Select experimentally informative, constraint-valid designs while separating mathematical feasibility, published observations, descriptive fits and unknown real-product performance. Better fitting is not therapeutic superiority.

## Computational acceptance

- API exactly 500 mg active-equivalent under stated potency assumption.
- Finished dry tablet 800 mg; exactly eight allowed excipients; unchanged bounds.
- Positive/finite masses, core+coat conservation, 100% ±0.1% and no near-duplicate designs.
- At least ten valid designs; complete mg and percent compositions.
- Source identity/table/time/mean/SD provenance and comparator mismatches documented.
- All compared models, held-out errors, unfavorable outcomes and missing endpoints retained.
- No invented early observations, stability results, manufacturing scores or efficacy ranking.
- Executable offline runner, regression tests and a publication-validation receipt.

## Laboratory acceptance — not achieved

Requires identified reference, approved method/criteria, controlled materials and actual replicated manufacture/assays. Source article endpoints are not the benchmark recipes' ground truth. Planned sampling, interaction estimability and raw f2 arithmetic cannot pass these gates.
