# T003 — Compare model chemistry with public literature

## Objective
Determine whether any generated route matches experimental literature and whether conditions, selectivity, yields, step counts and practicality support the proposed target preparation.

## Expected outcome
Paper titles, identifiers and links; explicit same-substrate versus analogous chemistry; condition/yield comparisons only where extracted evidence supports them; missing availability/selectivity marked unknown.

## Actual outcome
[Paper catalog](../benchmark/papers.md) contains protection and diaryl-ether papers (bibliographic/class-level evidence), an inspected public TBS-deprotection experiment, and two non-matching patent screening hits. The experimental 32% yield in the deprotection reference concerns another substrate. No exact target-route precedent or matched experimental yield was established. Literature coverage was incomplete and not structure-indexed; no novelty or freedom-to-operate claim follows.

[Comparison tables](../results/achievements_and_comparison.md) distinguish condition-tool predicted yields from retrosynthesis predicted yields. [Metrics](../benchmark/achieved_metrics.json) have null for measured target yield/selectivity and literature yield error. No claim of tool outperformance is supported.
