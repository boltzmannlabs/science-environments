## Draft matched comparison boundary

- One chemical target/form per comparison; final ≥95% purity and 100 g isolated material remain the planning specification, not an observed campaign result.
- Fix the same starting-material boundary (including advanced precursor preparation), assay-corrected isolated output definition and scale for candidate and literature baseline. Compare discovery experiments separately from 100 g projections.
- Primary endpoint to agree with owner: overall assay-corrected isolated yield at the agreed output specification. Alternative primary endpoint: fully scoped INR per compliant isolated gram. Do not switch endpoints after seeing results.
- Secondary endpoints: impurity identity/concentration and purge, isolated recovery, residual metals/solvents, isolation operations, process mass intensity, waste and work-up burden, reproducibility, and documented safety risks. No safety trade-off is automatically accepted for yield gain.
- Prespecify minimum meaningful improvement, repeat count/independence, measurement uncertainty, statistical analysis and exclusion/failure handling. Values remain unresolved until feasibility and variability data and owner decisions exist. Report all attempts and deviations, not just best batch.
- A superiority claim requires matched measured results with uncertainty supporting the prespecified margin, purity/safety gates passed, and the complete cost boundary. Model ranks/yields are not data for this comparison.

## Safety and scale/cost gate

A qualified review must assess reactive intermediates, incompatibilities, thermal/gas behavior, quench and waste, exposure/containment, mixing/mass transfer, catalyst/metal residues and isolation before authorized scale progression. A patent's reported discovery result is not sufficient safety qualification. Procurement costing requires supplier/date/currency/pack quantities, shipping/tax assumptions, actual yield/recovery scenarios, reaction/work-up/purification solvent, consumables/analytics, waste, and supported labour/utilities/contingency. No supplier quotes have been collected in this update and no INR total is calculable yet.
