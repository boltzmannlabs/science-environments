Current answer: no demonstrated improvement in chemistry, isolated yield, purity, cost, waste, safety or scale. Candidate enumeration and evidence bookkeeping are useful software achievements, not experimental outperformance.

## What “better than literature” should mean

Choose a primary outcome *before* experiments: e.g. higher isolated overall yield at identical purity and material boundary, lower total INR per compliant gram, lower process mass intensity/waste or a simpler independently reproducible isolation. Report trade-offs rather than a universal win. The required improvement magnitude, repeat count, uncertainty method and allowed safety/purity trade-offs are scientific decisions to agree with the process owner; they were not specified in this run and are not invented here.

Reference numbers are literature-reported observations (see benchmark papers): CN108794319B lists 82.3%, 60.3%, 95% stages and claimed 99.8% purity, with extraction inconsistencies; WO2013014448A1 Example 59 lists 76 mg/27% for a distinct original proposal. These are not transferable targets for unlike model pathways. No matched experimental error or improvement percentage can be calculated from current data.

## Proposed next decision

First recover the racemic parent artifacts and confirm authoritative structures and primary examples. Then choose one exact, feasible candidate and its literature baseline, agree on measurement/scale/cost boundaries, and obtain safety authorization for a controlled comparison. Until then, the correct result is an evidence-qualified hold—not an invented optimum.
