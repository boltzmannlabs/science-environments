# Achievements and limitations of the literature comparison

## Ibuprofen impurity A

12 generated routes and 38 reaction-labelled nodes were screened; all 12 target roots match the provisional target. The model export supplied no traceable step citations or procedure text. Literature review identified direct product descriptions in CN108794319B and WO2003043625A1. A top5 overlaps the CN Suzuki bond-forming family but changes the protected substrate. A top3 overlaps WO coupling/hydrogenation/hydrolysis topology but not the exact Stille reaction. A top1 free-acid methylation is not validated by the patent's ester methylation. These findings improve evidence classification, not synthetic performance. No lead was validated.

## Ibuprofen impurity M

7 generated routes and 21 reaction-labelled nodes were screened; all seven roots match the provisional target. No traceable node citations or procedure fields were present. M top4 shares a cyanohydrin feedstock class with Aramini et al. but must retain OH, unlike the cited deoxygenated product class. Bellezza's alpha-fluoro ester hydrolysis does not establish M formation. Kuchar and Ramesh/Reddy citations remain unresolved. No measured M yield, purity or cost advantage was demonstrated.

## Racemic ibuprofen

This is an artifact gap, not a statement that the task was unexecuted. Do not substitute impurity A or M rows for parent racemic ibuprofen.
