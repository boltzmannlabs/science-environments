# Literature benchmark

The existing OpenPKFlow 2.9.0 scenario output was checked against an author-reported F8 24-hour endpoint in Alladi Saritha and Rebecca Sanjana (2018), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1170150. At the paper's research F8 recipe and reported hardness, the unfitted first-order and Weibull scenario medians are 89.3809% and 89.4693%, versus the paper's 99.5% abstract claim; absolute discrepancies are 10.1191 and 10.0307 percentage points. The claim is above both assumed-scenario 95th percentiles (96.4847% and 96.5522%). These quantiles are not predictive intervals, and the article does not provide replicate-level numerical curves in the curated source. Treat this as a discrepancy/sanity check, not model validation or grounds for tuning on one point.

A second paper, Bharathi Arigela et al. (2018), DOI 10.5281/zenodo.1311884, was excluded from numerical scoring: Table 6's text extraction had missing/inconsistent cells and a nonmonotonic profile, and the original values could not be independently curated with sufficient confidence. The first paper's F8 Higuchi/Peppas R² values also conflict between Table 4 and discussion. Source PDF SHA-256 values matched the catalog; the PDFs are not included here.

Conclusion: the literature check argues against describing current scenario predictions as accurate. It does not establish that the paper is wrong, identify a recalled-lot cause, or validate a candidate. Product holdout benchmark remains `not_evaluated`; no measured Glenmark lot data were available.
