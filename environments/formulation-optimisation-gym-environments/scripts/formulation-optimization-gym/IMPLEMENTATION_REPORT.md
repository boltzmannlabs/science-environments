Implementation: `import_fda.py` validates exact recalled product, date, recall number, label SPL set ID and NDC, hashes the original read-only response snapshots in data/raw/, and imports source-linked claims into separate resumable episode JSONs under data/processed/. run.py performs read-only specialist triage, evidence audit and independent verifier; it abstains from prediction and experimental recommendations without prerequisites. No measurements, formula fractions, specification limits or causes were created.

Evidence gaps per case: controlled dissolution SOP/medium/apparatus/timepoints, approved acceptance criteria, lot-level measured dissolution profiles and analytical run metadata; batch manufacturing record/formula and incoming material tests; compression/coating/process/storage history; deviation/OOS investigation and documented regulatory permissions.
