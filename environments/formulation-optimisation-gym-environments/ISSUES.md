## Outcome at handoff

The latest executable checks passed **29/29 tests in the isolated OpenPKFlow environment**. The separate scenario mechanical audit found no arithmetic/ordering/label violations, but its status remains `mechanically_consistent_NOT_experimentally_validated`; the recalled-product scientific benchmark remains `not_evaluated`. No historical lot profile, root cause, improved recalled-product formulation, laboratory run or approved change was established. Issues G1–G2 and M1–M3 are the principal reasons that a software-success claim must not be read as scientific validation.
