# Ranking scope and uncertainty

The primary frozen Ridge order is a computational review priority, not a measured biological order. 14 of20 panel estimates exceed100 and are explicit extrapolations. Do not clip them into apparent measured inhibition. Raw endpoints differ across models and native/corrected OligoFormer share a checkpoint. Released-source nonmatches do not prove independent final-checkpoint exposure.

Seven methods are compared on the identical20-candidate panel. The five-domain combined comparator is frozen/exploratory, not trained or calibrated on current unmeasured targets. No target-specific measured-label accuracy, confidence interval or biological superiority is established. Near-ties should be treated as tiers.

Six panel entries have perfect outside-KRAS matches on a strand. They remain flagged comparisons, not KRAS-exclusive recommendations. The14 no-detected-perfect-match subset still has near-match/seed/reference/context uncertainty; no candidate is fully qualified. Shared transcript targeting does not establish mutation selectivity or sparing of normal-cell KRAS.
