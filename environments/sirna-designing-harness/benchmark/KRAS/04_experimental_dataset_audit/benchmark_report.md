# Independent experimental benchmark validation

## Outcome
BLOCKED_NO_AUDITABLE_INDEPENDENT_DATASET. Dataset audit completed; independent split, inference and metric stages were not executed. This is not evidence that models perform poorly or well: the intended independent comparison is not identifiable from the audited inputs. No candidate generation, folding, transcriptome screening, model inference, refitting, cloud execution or network acquisition was performed.

## Dataset discovery and source provenance
Local sources were identified from retained provenance manifests and actual data files, including original supplements for Shabalina2006, Sciabola2013, Monopoli2023 and Davis2025, the Figshare siRNAEfficacyDB compilation and released Hu/Mix/Taka model-reference data. Additional installed GMM-siRNA and siRNA-Features data were inspected. This is a bounded local audit, not an exhaustive literature/filesystem census. Source URLs, licenses, original/derivative distinctions and hashes are in dataset_manifest.json and input_hashes.json.

## Duplicate and label-scale audit
Exact full-sequence strand-equivalence, conservative19nt overlap windows, cross-source matches and conflicting labels within available endpoint/context fields were audited. Distinct doses or reporter/native endpoints are not automatically duplicates. Incomplete context means different labels are not automatically a corruption finding. The known44-row repeated-duplex LaminA/Harborth issue remains quarantined by the historical source flags; no replacement duplexes or efficacy values were invented. Source %Inhibition is not silently equated with mRNA knockdown. Remaining-activity/control/reporter endpoints remain explicit;100-minus-source is only a documented arithmetic complement. Normalized released-model labels differ from raw experimental percent scales; see label_scale_audit.json. No test-label normalization or calibration was fitted.

## Why independence is blocked
Shabalina is a classic compilation with sequence/development overlap and incomplete per-row original-study/context curation. Sciabola was examined during RN.Ai-Predict development, and21nt designs are not automatically equivalent to19nt model inputs. Davis and Monopoli have modified/scaffold-specific20nt or asymmetric constructs outside the verified unmodified19nt protocol; zero sequence matches does not resolve checkpoint exposure or domain mismatch. SiaRNA2026 is a reuse of classic source datasets, not a new measured screen. GMM data can be LFS pointer files rather than available measurements; siRNA-Features expression/count or coordinate tables are not automatically per-construct on-target percent-silencing labels.

## Predictor training provenance
Released OligoFormer/RNAiSpline Hu/Mix/Taka references were hash-verified and compared to the candidate sources. They are training/development material, not independent validation data. RN.Ai-Predict final-checkpoint membership is UNKNOWN, never reported as zero exposure. Ridge has an auditable2410-record original training partition; it is not falsely assigned unknown training. However, that does not establish an independent, fully curated, untouched original-study/target test in the external compilations or certify pretrained predictors. The all-model common independent evaluation gate therefore remains blocked. Model-specific constraints are retained; no claim is made that every conceivable Ridge-only external test is impossible.

## Evaluation design and metrics
No new train/development/test assignments were created. Historical random, publication-group and target-group folds are preserved as historical evidence, not a newly untouched test. A new split cannot erase frozen-model historical development exposure. split_manifest.json states future original-study and duplicate-component grouping, target holding/purging and test sealing. per_record_predictions.csv is an explicit header-only independent-prediction schema because no test records were eligible; measurement records remain fully auditable in the eligibility table. metric_summary.csv uses null results and zero attempts, not zero performance.

Future comparisons should use matched study/target/assay/dose/time/chemistry pools. Spearman/Pearson assess measured-endpoint prediction only when labels and scales are defined. Precision@K requires a prespecified assay hit rule; NDCG@K requires a justified nonnegative relevance definition. MAE/RMSE require valid shared units, not class probabilities or priority scores treated as percentages. Probability calibration requires matching class definitions. Compare with training-only mean and GC baselines and random ordering of real labels; no synthetic efficacy ground truth. Uncertainty should respect original-study clusters and adequate study counts; no CIs are invented here. Full Bench/combined validation additionally requires actual compatible full-feature evidence, not an efficacy-only replacement.

## Interpretation boundary
Experimental efficacy prediction, within-pool ranking, generalization to unseen studies/targets, computational sequence specificity and construct qualification are separate questions. The present gate failure resolves none as positive or negative model performance. KRAS candidate agreement/specificity does not become measured validation by this audit. Existing KRAS shared-arm completion and biological PARTIAL status are unchanged. The mutation arm remains BLOCKED_PENDING_MUTATION_SPECIFICATION; no hypothetical mutant was constructed. Nothing here establishes safety, immune tolerance, delivery or mutation selectivity.

## Evidence required to proceed
Obtain a source-verified compatible experimental sequence/context/construct dataset with individual assay and original-study metadata, plus final-checkpoint training/development/model-selection exclusion evidence (or author attestation). Resolve transcript versions, strand/length representation and chemistry without arbitrary trimming. Freeze endpoints, hit/gain definitions, development-only mappings and grouped splits before test access. Alternatively preregister a distinct modified-siRNA out-of-domain study with appropriate models and claims. No additional model run is justified merely to fill empty independent metrics.
