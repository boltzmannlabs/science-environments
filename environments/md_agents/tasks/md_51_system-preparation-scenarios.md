# MD-51 — Molecular System Preparation Scenarios

Detect missing residues/atoms, incorrect names or numbering, ligand bond-order or stereochemistry errors, protonation/tautomer problems, missing cofactors/metals/waters, and invalid boxes or solvent. Require input/topology hashes and an independent validation report. Do not invent or silently alter inputs; return PASS, FAIL, or REVIEW with evidence.
