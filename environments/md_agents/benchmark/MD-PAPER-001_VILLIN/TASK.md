# MD-PAPER-001 task

Reconstruct the paper's force field, solvent, temperature, pressure,
electrostatics, timestep, equilibration, production length, replicas, and
folding/native-state observables. Label every field `REPORTED`, `INFERRED`,
`NOT_REPORTED`, or `CONFLICTING`.

Required outputs are a structured protocol, provenance and input hashes,
missing-data record, bounded execution configuration, and independent
comparison plan. Scientific qualification remains `REVIEW` until paper-matched
trajectories and uncertainty evidence are available.
