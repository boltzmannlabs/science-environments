## Study (primary source)
- **Paper:** De novo design of epitope-specific antibodies via a structure-driven
  computational workflow (tFold system). Nature Communications, online December 2025,
  volume 17 (2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-67361-9. PMC: PMC12815924.
- **Full text retrieved:** NCBI eutils efetch, `db=pmc&id=12815924&rettype=full&retmode=xml`
  (252,742 bytes, 2026-11-10; working copy
  /home/boltzmann10/.sci/cache/scratch/pmc_efetch.xml — scratch, not durable).
- **From the paper (Table 1, extracted character-for-character):** the 8 framework
  region sequences and CDR length declarations → `sources/framework_table1.fasta`.
- **H965:** named in the paper as the highest-affinity anti-PD-1 design (final-design
  comparator). Its paired VH/VL sequences were verified against the staged reference
  complex (below); the paper body confirms the IGHV3-23/IGKV1-5 framework and an
  8-residue CDR-H3. Supplementary Table 6 (sequence listing) was not present in the
  PMC full-text XML (supplementary files are linked, not embedded); the staged
  reference complex is the verification artifact.
- **D12:** published anti-PD-1 sequence comparator (CDR-H3 length 9 per paper).
  Not a design input. Full D12 sequence not staged here (not required for the design;
  sequence-distance comparison against D12 is optional pending retrieval).

## Target construct
- **PDB:** 4ZQK — "Structure of the complex of human programmed death-1 (PD-1) and its
  ligand PD-L1" (RCSB). Retrieved 2026-11-10 from
  https://files.rcsb.org/download/4ZQK.pdb (working copy in scratch).
- **Chain A:** PD-L1 (UniProt Q9NZQ7), residues 18-132 (115 aa) — ligand for epitope
  computation only, never a design target.
- **Chain B:** PD-1 (UniProt Q15116, legacy entry; current Q5W5W0), residues 33-146
  (106 aa) — the design target. Sequence → `sources/pd1_4zqk_chainB.fasta`.
- **RCSB API check:** data.rcsb.org polymer-entity endpoints confirm entity 1 = Q9NZQ7
  (PD-L1), entity 2 = Q15116 (PD-1).
- **Mature-protein mapping:** PD-1 precursor IgV domain; the deposited construct omits
  the disordered D/E-loop segment `DRSQPGQDS` and ends 4 residues before the
  canonical sequence's `...RVTERRAE` tail (`...RAELRVTE`). This omission is a property
  of the crystal construct, not an editing choice.

## Epitope
- **Definition:** PD-1 ligand-binding surface = chain-B residues with any heavy atom
  within 6.0 Å of any PD-L1 (chain A) heavy atom, computed on native 4ZQK.
- **Result:** 28 residues (4ZQK numbering) B:64, 66, 68, 70-71, 73-78, 81, 84,
  122-136 → `sources/epitope_pd1_ligand_binding.json`.
- **Interpretation limit:** overlap with this surface supports a competition
  hypothesis, not demonstrated functional blockade.

## Structural conditioning (stage 0)
- **Artifact:** evidence/reference/task003_h965_pd1.pdb (from the previous local run,
  Boltz2-predicted H965(VH)+H965(VL)+PD-1 complex; chains A=VH, B=VL, C=PD-1).
- **Use:** source of the framework-only design input. VH/VL framework atoms (exact
  Table-1 FR sequences) are retained; ALL CDR atoms are removed. The PD-1 chain is
  retained in the same coordinate frame as the target. No H965 CDR sequence or
  identity enters any candidate.
- **Disclosure:** the conditioning pose derives from the tFold reference design
  (H965 is a control, not a parent); this is the only study-provided structural
  context for the framework + PD-1 pair.
