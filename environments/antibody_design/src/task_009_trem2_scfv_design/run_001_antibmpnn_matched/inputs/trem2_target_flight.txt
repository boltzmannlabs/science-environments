4c:T463,TREM2 is a receptor on microglia, the brain's resident immune cells. It detects fats and fat-carrying particles released by damaged tissue, including components of dying neurons, damaged myelin and amyloid. Through it, microglia survive, move toward damage and clear debris. It carries no signalling machinery of its own and works through partner chains, so switching it on requires clustering several receptors together rather than simply occupying one.

Its genetic support is the strongest of any brain-immune target: a rare variant called R47H substantially raises Alzheimer's risk, with published estimates ranging from under twofold to above fourfold, and losing both copies causes a rare inherited dementia accompanied by bone cysts. The clinic has not followed. The activating antibody AL002 missed its main goal and every secondary goal in a Phase 2 Alzheimer's trial despite clearly engaging the target, and caused unexpected brain-imaging changes. Alector halted the programme after the November 2024 results and AbbVie ended the partnership in January 2025. 56:T463,TREM2 is a receptor on microglia, the brain's resident immune cells. It detects fats and fat-carrying particles released by damaged tissue, including components of dying neurons, damaged myelin and amyloid. Through it, microglia survive, move toward damage and clear debris. It carries no signalling machinery of its own and works through partner chains, so switching it on requires clustering several receptors together rather than simply occupying one.

Its genetic support is the strongest of any brain-immune target: a rare variant called R47H substantially raises Alzheimer's risk, with published estimates ranging from under twofold to above fourfold, and losing both copies causes a rare inherited dementia accompanied by bone cysts. The clinic has not followed. The activating antibody AL002 missed its main goal and every secondary goal in a Phase 2 Alzheimer's trial despite clearly engaging the target, and caused unexpected brain-imaging changes. Alector halted the programme after the November 2024 results and AbbVie ended the partnership in January 2025.
