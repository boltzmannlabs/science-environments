# Coarse-Grained MD Status

**Lifecycle: In Progress — executable toy profile and partially validated.**

The package contains 20 CG contracts, uniform task cards, deterministic
runtime checks, an OpenMM bead-spring smoke calculation, and harness evidence.
It does not claim qualification of all CG force fields, membranes, LLPS,
parameterization, or long-timescale biomolecular systems.

Family-specific and reference-dependent checks remain `REVIEW` until mapping,
force-field, replicas, observables, and compatible reference conditions are
independently verified.
