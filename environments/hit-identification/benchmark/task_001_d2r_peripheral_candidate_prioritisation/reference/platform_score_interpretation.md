# Binding Affinity Energy interpretation

Source: installed Boltzmann connector owner-supplied product guide; not a live service verification. Guide SHA256: `c2817d083aeb5642b5865cd0720129408464d925dbd48cc97e06909969dff8bc`.

The guide identifies global_binding_affinity as a SMILES/protein-sequence NESSO scoring tool. Lower scores indicate stronger predicted binding within comparable runs. It supplies no physical energy units and does not establish conversion to an experimental affinity constant. The retained CSV contains no unit field. Its values are therefore reported as scores with unestablished physical units.
