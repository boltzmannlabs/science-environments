## What we achieved compared with literature

The tool enumerated alternative disconnections and the screening identified which share literature reaction families. Primary patents provide stronger direct A-product evidence than the uncited model exports. No improved isolated yield, purity, experimental step count, safety, cost or 100 g feasibility was demonstrated. The complete [A/M route inventory](../results/screening_routes.csv) and [measured-versus-computational metrics](achieved_metrics.json) retain this distinction.
