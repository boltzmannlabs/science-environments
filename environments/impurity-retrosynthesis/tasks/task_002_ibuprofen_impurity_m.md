# Task: Ibuprofen EP impurity M

## Scientific question

Identify chemically credible synthesis routes for Ibuprofen EP impurity M, review generated paths against literature, and assess the planning objective of 100 g isolated material at ≥95% purity with source-backed INR cost. Identity context: 60057-62-7. Confirm connectivity, regioisomer, stereochemistry and form before ranking.

## Inputs and work performed

Current evidence: 7 paths / 21 reaction-labelled nodes. Literature comparators: Bellezza 2005 DOI 10.1016/j.tet.2005.06.007; Aramini 2009 DOI 10.1016/j.tet.2009.01.005; unverified Kuchar 1984 and Ramesh/Reddy 2007 fragments. Inputs must include authorized route export, reviewed target SMILES, complete reference identifiers and any experimental/supplier data. Model-generation completion is distinct from experimental synthesis.

## Work to perform

Test the documentary claim that M top4 can retain alpha-OH during nitrile hydrolysis; compare M top2 free-hydroxy-acid coupling against exact-substrate evidence, not generic names.

Preserve every original route ID; audit structures and complete reaction atom sources; distinguish exact vs analogous precedent; assess selectivity, purification, hazards, availability and scale. Obtain dated INR pack quotes and defensible yield/recovery scenarios before costing. If chemistry/evidence is inadequate, document a hold with the exact missing input rather than forcing a winner.

## Expected deliverables and acceptance

1. Identity and source audit with canonical target representation and unresolved form/stereo questions.
2. All-route comparison with original IDs, intermediates, disconnections, evidence citations, model-only field labels and route-specific risks.
3. Justified lead/backup OR explicit absence of a defensible recommendation.
4. Stepwise 100 g material balance, low/base/high INR and INR/g with sources, dates and assumptions OR explicit not-estimable decision.
5. Computational checks separated from planned/actual wet-lab validation; no claim of measured yield or ≥95% purity without real data.
6. Like-for-like literature comparison and archived output links with numerical metrics only where backed by artifacts.
