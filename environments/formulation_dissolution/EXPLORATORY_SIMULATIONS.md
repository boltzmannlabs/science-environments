This is an EXPLORATORY mass-balance window, not an approved material, clinical, stability, excipient-safety or manufacturing-constraint space.
For t in hours, OpenPKFlow forms are first order Q=100[1−exp(−k t)] and Weibull Q=100[1−exp(−(t/tau)^alpha)]. For each model, assumed independent uniform ranges include first-order k=0.08–0.12 h^-1, Weibull tau=7.5–11 h and alpha=0.75–1.05. Assumed log-rate effects: Compritol −0.7 to +0.7 per 35 mg, Carbopol −0.6 to +0.6 per 56 mg, hardness slows release 0–0.2 per kg/cm². These rates/effects are NOT measured or fitted; in particular both signs for polymer effects are possible. The toy acceptance windows Q(2 h)≤25% and Q(24 h)≥85% are invented design goals, NOT FDA or product QC limits. Scenario pass fraction measures how often BOTH toy goals hold under these arbitrarily specified uniform draws: it is not a calibrated probability of passing QC. Arbitrary unitless ranking score = 0.75 × scenario pass fraction + 0.15 × mean clipped margins + 0.10 × lower-polymer-mass proxy. Sensitivity and comparator checks
At fixed assumed mid-model conditions and a positive log-rate effect, increasing Compritol 70→105 mg in the model changes Q(2 h) by +10.62 and Q(24 h) by +11.97 percentage points; assuming the opposite, equally unverified sign changes those differences to −10.62 and −11.97. Changing Carbopol 14→70 mg similarly gives either sign; increasing assumed hardness 7.5→9.5 kg/cm² changes Q(2 h) by −3.53 and Q(24 h) by −4.01 points under its stipulated slowing coefficient. These are properties of the ASSUMPTION, not estimated chemical effects.
For an apples-to-apples scenario-only conventional comparator, research F8 at its paper-reported hardness 9.3 has scalar score 0.4917 and joint-goal fraction 0.5859. Yet the paper's F8 abstract reports 99.5% at 24 h, whereas the toy first-order and Weibull F8 median 24 h predictions were respectively 89.38% and 89.47% (absolute discrepancies 10.12 and 10.03 percentage points). This mismatch flags lack of calibration; comparing one author-reported endpoint does NOT validate, refute or rank dissolution models.
