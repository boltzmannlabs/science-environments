# Our stability predictions: RML

These are the real DDGun stability checks run after the original candidates were selected. They support the paper comparison but do not change the original workflow outcome or qualification.
`predictions.csv` contains the final combined-variant values; `input.json` and `output.txt` retain exact submitted inputs and returned results. `job.json` records identity, timing, method, hashes and the actual completed job.
DDGun predicts unfolding energy change in kcal/mol, positive for stabilization. It is a different method from the paper's predictors. [Comparison report](../../../../../benchmark/task_001_rml_stability/comparisions/benchmark_report.docx).
