# Environment implementation and recorded work

## Implemented

Six numbered tasks, published reference notes, per-task result folders and a Markdown report builder with embedded figures. Original candidate sequences and numerical outputs are retained with their execution records. Reference-property predictions are complete for the reconstructable variants: 99 requested Tm, raw pHPred and NetSolP values were verified across 33 sequence records.

Additional open-source metric comparisons are available: 17 unique EvoEF1 whole-variant folding predictions, two new DDGun predictions with compatible completed results reused, and four matched static PACKMAN entropy calculations. Original published values and matched recalculations are labeled separately.

## Recorded campaign outcomes

As of 16 November 2026, all ten native arms are operationally closed under the owner's terminal-outcome policy. Sun and Zhang computational executions and available-output comparisons are retained. Original design arms include unsuccessful or inconclusive outcomes with unresolved assessments; closing a run does not qualify a candidate. Each task's results includes its recorded outcome.

The separate AtHOL1 assessment completed three 30 ns trajectories, 750 field snapshots and 75 explicit C–I localized-orbital calculations. Its numerical export is included with source labels, identities and a matched comparison figure.

Assisted execution exports are available for RML, original LEH, Sun LEH and Zhang hydrolases, with their own sequences, scores, workflow JPGs and outcome summaries. BLA and AtHOL1 have agent-alone executions only.
