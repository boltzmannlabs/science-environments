# FEP/TI Agents Status

**Lifecycle: In Progress — executable toy calculation and fixture validation.**

The package contains 15 environment-family contracts, 15 specialist
contracts, 10 problem-solving agents, uniform task cards, a toy TI evaluator,
and a SAMPL6 provenance manifest.

The toy harmonic calculation and manifest checks do not constitute a qualified
protein–ligand FEP result. Real qualification requires molecular topologies,
parameters, window trajectories, overlap, convergence, uncertainty, cycle
consistency, and independent repeats.
