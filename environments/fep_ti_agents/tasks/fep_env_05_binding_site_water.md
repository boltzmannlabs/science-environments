# FEP-ENV-05 — Binding-Site Water and Structural Rearrangement

**Purpose:** Test whether trapped waters or slow pocket rearrangements bias a transformation.

**Inputs:** complex trajectories, water occupancy/contacts, pocket conformations, replicas, and reference interaction criteria.

**Procedure:** cluster pocket states, compare hydration occupancy and ligand contacts, and assess whether all relevant states were sampled.

**Acceptance:** PASS requires reproducible relevant-state populations. Unresolved slow states or missing water sampling are REVIEW, not a forced affinity result.
