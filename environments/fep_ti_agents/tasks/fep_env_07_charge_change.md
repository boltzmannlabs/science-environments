# FEP-ENV-07 — Charge-Changing Transformations

**Purpose:** Qualify transformations with a net charge change and electrostatic finite-size corrections.

**Inputs:** charged endpoints, box/ionic conditions, correction model, and correction uncertainty.

**Procedure:** verify charge balance, correction applicability, box-size assumptions, and sensitivity to the correction.

**Acceptance:** PASS requires documented correction assumptions and sensitivity within tolerance; otherwise REVIEW.
