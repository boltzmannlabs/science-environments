# FEP03 — published protein–ligand RBFE reconstruction

**Task family:** FEP/TI paper benchmark  
**Reference:** Ross et al. 2023 public protein–ligand RBFE benchmark  
**Status:** catalogued; edge selection pending

## Objective

Select and freeze one protein–ligand transformation other than the PTP1B
FEP01 edge, then evaluate general, specialist and orchestrated agents on the
same paper-derived inputs. The selected edge must have identifiable endpoint
structures, mapping, protocol metadata and reference evidence before execution.

## Stop condition

Until a concrete edge and source revision are frozen, no topology, lambda
trajectory or free-energy result may be reported for FEP03.
