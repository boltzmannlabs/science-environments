Decision rules (applied together to all input cells, with every failed rule listed):
  detected genes < 200: 0 cells;
  detected genes > 2500: 5 cells;
  mitochondrial percentage > 5: 57 cells.
The 200-gene floor screens severely low-complexity cells; all vendor-filtered
barcodes already exceed it (observed minimum 212).
The upper 2500-gene cutoff flags the extreme high-complexity tail (99th
percentile 1749.01), a conservative potential-multiplet
flag; high-complexity real cells can also be excluded. The 5% MT cutoff is
above the observed 95th percentile (4.015%) and screens
unusually mitochondria-rich, potentially stressed/damaged cells; this is not
a measured viability assay. No additional UMI cutoff is applied because
complexity and mt% suffice and the observed min UMI count is
548; UMI counts are reported for auditing.
