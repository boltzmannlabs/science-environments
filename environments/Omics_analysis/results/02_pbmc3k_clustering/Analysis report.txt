PBMC 3K unsupervised clustering (no cell-type annotation)
Cells: 2595; genes: 13714; input HVGs: 2000.

Methods: The input X is preprocessed log-normalized expression; the original X
and counts layer were retained. The provided HVG subset was centered and
unit-variance scaled (clipped to +/-10 for PCA only). ARPACK PCA fit 50 PCs.
A 15-nearest-neighbor graph was constructed using the first 40 PCs and the
Euclidean metric. Undirected igraph-flavor Leiden was run for two iterations
at resolution 0.7, random seed 42. UMAP of that graph used minimum distance
0.5 and seed 42. UMAP axes are visualization coordinates, not independent
confirmation of cluster separation.

Resolution selection (internal diagnostic only, no labels or reference):
On the same 40-PC graph, candidate resolutions 0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 1.1, 1.3, 1.5
were compared using cluster sizes, sampled PC-space silhouette and agreement
between Leiden seeds 42 and 43. 0.5 gave 7 clusters (sizes 13 to 1172),
0.7 gave 8 clusters (sizes 13 to 627) with seed ARI ~0.966; 0.9 gave
8 clusters with seed ARI ~0.859; higher resolutions gave 9-12 clusters,
including a 3-cell partition at 1.3, and lower seed consistency at 1.5.
Chosen resolution 0.7 retains two small groups without the large 1172-cell
cluster or the 3-cell partition. This is a pragmatic unsupervised choice,
not evidence of a predetermined cell-type count. Actual selected-run
seed ARI: 0.966; PC-space silhouette (1500-cell random sample): 0.097.

Cluster sizes (cell counts): 0: 565, 1: 325, 2: 460, 3: 410, 4: 627, 5: 163, 6: 32, 7: 13.
Limitations: no cell-type labels assigned, no external reference used, and
cluster stability across independent biological samples is not established.
The smallest clusters merit follow-up examination for technical artifacts.
